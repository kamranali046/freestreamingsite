Frequently asked questions

What is torrent streaming?

It plays a torrent's video in your browser as it downloads, so you can watch immediately instead of waiting for the file to finish.

Do I need a VPN for torrent streaming?

A VPN is strongly recommended because P2P torrenting shares your IP address with others in the swarm.

Is torrent streaming safe?

Torrent sites are ad-heavy. Use an ad-blocker, avoid downloading unknown files, and follow the laws where you live.
